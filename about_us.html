Apex Arena started with one simple idea: booking a court should take a minute, not three phone calls. Here is who we are and what we offer.

Our story

From one court to four sports

Apex Arena began as a single futsal field. Players kept asking the same questions: what does it cost, are you open, is the 8 PM slot free? Answers lived in a notebook, and double bookings were common.

Today the complex has twelve premium courts across badminton, basketball, futsal and volleyball, and every answer is on this website. Walk-ins are welcome, but most of our regulars book from their phones.

Our mission

Easy to find. Fair to pay. Simple to book.

Give students, young adults and local teams in Karachi a clean, safe place to play, with honest availability and one clear price for every hour.

Play fair, stay safe

Arena rules and guidelines

Arrive 10 minutes early with your booking ID. Slots end on time for the next team.
Wear the right shoes for your court. Metal studs and outdoor shoes are never allowed.
No food on the courts. Water bottles are fine.
Treat staff, referees and other players with respect.
Players under 16 need a guardian on site.
Report any injury or damaged equipment to the front desk straight away.
