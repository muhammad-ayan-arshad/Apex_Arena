About this court

Our futsal fields use FIFA-approved turf over a shock-absorbing layer, with rebound boards around the edge so the ball stays in play. The floodlights make late evening games feel like match night.

Book a field for league fixtures, friendly matches or a regular weekly game. Match balls and bibs are included with every booking.
