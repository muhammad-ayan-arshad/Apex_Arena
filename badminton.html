Badminton Synthetic Courts

About this court

Our badminton hall has four indoor courts with a cushioned synthetic surface that is kind to knees and ankles. The ceiling is high enough for full clears, and the side lighting keeps the shuttle easy to see from every angle.

Book a court for a relaxed singles game, a doubles session with friends, or regular practice. Rackets and shuttles can be hired at the front desk if you forgot yours.

Play fair, stay safe

Court rules

Wear clean, non-marking indoor shoes only.
Maximum 4 players on a court at one time.
Keep water bottles on the side bench, not on the court.
Dispose of broken shuttles in the bin provided.
Arrive 10 minutes early. Your slot ends on time for the next team.
