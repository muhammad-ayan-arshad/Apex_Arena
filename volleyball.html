Indoor Beach Volleyball

About this court

Serve, dig and dive on soft, deep sand inside a climate-comfortable hall. The nets are adjustable, the lines are clearly marked, and the lighting keeps the ball easy to track.

Courts suit casual games, team practice and social tournaments. Volleyballs can be borrowed from the front desk.

Play fair, stay safe

Court rules

Play barefoot or in sand socks. Outdoor shoes stay off the sand.
Maximum 12 players per court (4v4 or 6v6).
Remove jewellery before playing.
Rinse sand off at the foot station before leaving.
Arrive 10 minutes early. Your slot ends on time for the next team.
