About this court

Play on a full-size court built to NBA proportions, with proper rims and padded stanchions. The surface is smooth and consistent, so dribbling and cutting feel the same from baseline to baseline.

Courts are available by the hour for pick-up games, team practice or friendly matches. A digital scoreboard and shot clock are included, and balls and bibs are available at the desk.
